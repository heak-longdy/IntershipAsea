For Interns

1. Easy Application Process

Simple Online Booking: Our user-friendly website allows you to browse through a diverse range of internship opportunities and apply with ease. You can search by industry, company, and location to find the perfect match for your skills and interests.

Optional Consultation: To ensure the best fit, we offer an optional consultation with a British national residing in Cambodia. This personalized session helps you understand the specifics of the internship, cultural expectations, and daily life in Cambodia.

2. Pre-Departure Support

Flight and Visa Arrangements: We assist you with booking flights and handling visa applications, ensuring all necessary documents are in order.

Pre-Departure Orientation: We provide detailed pre-departure information, including cultural insights, packing tips, and travel advice to prepare you for your journey.

3. Post-Arrival Assistance

Airport Pickup: Upon arrival in Cambodia, you will be greeted by our team and provided with transportation to your accommodation.

Accommodation Arrangements: We offer a range of housing options to suit different preferences and budgets, ensuring you have a comfortable place to stay.

4. In-Country Support

Orientation Program: We conduct a comprehensive orientation program to help you acclimate to your new environment. This includes language basics, cultural norms, and practical information about living in Cambodia.

24/7 Support: Our team is available around the clock to provide assistance and address any concerns you may have during your stay.

Networking Opportunities: We organize events and activities to help you network with other interns, professionals, and local communities, enhancing your overall experience.

5. Post-Internship Support

Career Guidance: We offer career counseling and support to help you leverage your internship experience in your future career.

Certificate of Completion: Upon successful completion of your internship, you will receive a certificate recognizing your achievements and contributions.
